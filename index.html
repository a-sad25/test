How NexusFlow Works

⚡

Ingest & Stream

Real-time ingestion of GPS telemetry, weather APIs, port congestion feeds, carrier EDI, and IoT sensor data at 50,000+ events/second.

Multi-model ensemble scores every shipment leg continuously — detecting bottleneck signatures, delay probability, and cascade risk before they materialize.
